Coordinates

Every square on a chessboard has a name. This makes it possible to describe moves and positions exactly.

  • Ranks are the horizontal rows, numbered from 1 to 8.
  • Files are the vertical columns, lettered from a to h.

A square's coordinate is its file followed by its rank: e4, d5, a1, h8. The board below is shown from White's side, so a1 is in the bottom left corner.
